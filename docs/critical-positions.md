# Critical positions

- PGN tags `[%crit opening]`, `[%crit middlegame]`, and `[%crit endgame]` become blue, red, and green diamonds beside the annotated move, with phase tooltips.
- Previous/next buttons visit marked positions in score order, including variations, and stop at either end. Illegal moves are excluded from navigation.
- Recognized tags replace their generated commentary text; unknown values remain readable.
- Markers are study bookmarks, not automatic exercises. Open **Piece mode** to explore freely, then return to the selected position.
- The original PGN and archive remain unchanged.
